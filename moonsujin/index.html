01 / Biography

About the artist.

Moon Sujin is a Seoul-based R&B singer-songwriter whose work moves between independent R&B, Korean hip-hop and major K-pop releases. Her voice appears on EXO-SC's 1 Billion Views, while her writing and vocal credits extend to projects by j-hope, Jay Park, WOODZ and Simon Dominic. Alongside her own music, she has built a growing record of cross-border collaborations with ØZI, Karencici and GANGGA.
