TUIDE · COMMERCIAL PROFILE · SEP 2026
PROFILE THESIS

A new-generation girl group with a defined point of view from day one.

TUIDE is the first seven-member girl group from ABD, a new HYBE label, with member backgrounds spanning Korea, the United States, the United Kingdom and Japan. Debuting in August 2026, the group arrived with a distinct visual language and a clearly articulated “Soultronic” sound. The opportunity is not a blank canvas. It is a group with a point of view whose long-term brand architecture is still relatively open.

Effortless visual chemistry. Refined musical identity.
Early fashion validation.

04 · PARTNERSHIP PATH

Start with chemistry. Build toward ownership of the relationship.

01Shows & Events

Fashion week, runway shows, brand dinners, openings and cultural moments — a low-friction first step to establish authentic chemistry.

02Branded Editorial / Special Cover

Fashion or beauty editorials, special covers and visual projects that allow the house and the group to test creative alignment.

03Seasonal Campaign

Group or selected-member campaigns across Korea, Japan or broader Asia once visual and strategic fit is established.

04Capsule / Creative Collaboration

Limited product, capsule, content or cultural projects built around TUIDE's color, sound and individual member identities.

05Ambassadorship / Long-term Category Partnership

For the right house or category, early creative alignment can evolve into a member or group-level long-term relationship with genuine first-mover value.

FOR THE BRANDDiscovery value before the choice becomes obvious.

A credible partner can build association while TUIDE's long-term brand architecture is still forming — with more room to influence the visual story than with an established act.

FOR TUIDEThe right house can accelerate cultural legitimacy.

A selective early relationship can strengthen TUIDE's fashion language and global image without over-commercializing a group that is still establishing its artistic identity.
